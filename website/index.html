Meet Ms. Megan 👋

Hi, I'm Megan Yamauchi — "Ms. Megan"! I'm an experienced teacher with a Master's in Early Childhood Education and nearly 15 years working with children, and I taught in the Dysart and Peoria Unified School Districts before opening The Tot Spot.

I come from a family of educators, and teaching has always felt like a natural calling. I'm also a mom to two little ones — Luke and Lillie — so I know just how precious your kiddos are.

I created The Tot Spot to be a safe, nurturing, education-focused place where little minds can learn, grow, and thrive.

Our Program

Hands-on, play-based learning that develops the whole child.

🎨

Preschool Prep Curriculum

Early literacy, pre-writing, letters & numbers, scissor skills, and art exploration.

💛

Social-Emotional Learning

Kindness, patience, self-regulation, following routines, and expressing feelings.

👧

Small Classes

Just 6 students, with small-group and 1:1 instruction so every child is seen.

🧒

Ages 2.5 – 4

A gentle, gradual introduction to a school routine. Potty training encouraged, not required.

🩹

CPR & First Aid Certified

A safe, well-maintained, childproofed home environment with a LifeVac on hand.

📅

Part- or Full-Time

Mon/Wed or Tues/Thurs, or Mon–Thurs full-time. 8:00–11:30 AM. Aligned to the PUSD calendar.
